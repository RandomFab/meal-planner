# Examples

A fictional household, and what Meal Planner does for it. Exact wording varies from one conversation to the next: these show the shape of the answers, not a script.

- [`household.md`](household.md): the household's profile, as setup writes it.
- [`week.md`](week.md): a weekly menu, the shopping list that follows, and a change of plan.
- [`recipe.md`](recipe.md): a recipe sent as a photo, with a strict restriction flagged.

Everything here is made up: names, shops and habits.
