# A recipe from a photo

The household sends a photo of this handwritten card:

> **Peanut noodles**, serves 4, 20 min
> 400 g egg noodles · 3 tbsp peanut butter · 2 tbsp soy sauce · 1 tbsp honey · 1 lime · 1 carrot · 1 handful roasted peanuts

Peanuts are a strict restriction in the [profile](household.md), so Claude flags them before creating anything:

> **Claude:** ⚠️ This recipe contains peanuts (peanut butter and roasted peanuts), which are on your "Never" list. I won't add it as it is.
> I can save an adapted version: sesame paste instead of peanut butter, toasted sesame seeds instead of peanuts. Shall I?
> I help, but I'm not a medical tool: always check the labels.

Once approved, the dish is saved with its ingredients converted to one adult portion and linked to existing products (Carrot, Egg noodles…), without duplicates.
